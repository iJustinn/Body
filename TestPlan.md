# Body Test Plan

Generated 2026-08-30 against branch `body-v1.0.2` (app version 1.0.2 build 1).

> Apple Watch targets (`BodyWatch`, `BodyWatchWidgetExtension`) and the shared `BodyWatchShared` group are new in 0.9.3; on-watch realtime compute is new in 0.9.10. `ProjectConfigurationTests` asserts their configuration (bundle identifiers, versions, App Group + HealthKit entitlements); the shared-code compute pipeline (splice → readiness recompute → snapshot build, and the phone/watch merge rules) has automated cross-path parity coverage in `BodyTests/WatchComputeParityTests.swift`, `WatchComputeMergeTests.swift`, `WatchDeltaSplicerTests.swift`, and `WatchComputeSeedTests.swift`; on-device watch behavior itself is not yet covered by automated cases. The watch is no longer display-only: alongside the metrics snapshot the iPhone pushes over WatchConnectivity, the phone attaches a compressed compute seed (recent trends, sleep history, and Training Load history); the watch persists that seed (a phone republish carrying identical data is ignored — the seed's transport timestamp is not part of its encoded identity — so a redundant push can't discard an in-flight on-watch compute) and, on a **foreground-only** trigger (opening the app, the app becoming active, or tapping the refresh button — never a background refresh), re-queries a short recent window from its own HealthKit, splices it onto the seeded history, and recomputes Readiness, Sleep, Heart Rate, HRV, Resting Heart Rate, Training Load, and Skin Temperature through the same scoring code the iPhone uses. The displayed snapshot merges **freshest-wins per metric**: an on-watch value is adopted only when it is newer than what's on screen, and a later phone push never rolls back a fresher on-watch value (or vice versa) — an on-watch compute never alters the phone's own publish sequencing. Heart Rate and HRV additionally keep their pre-existing live local refresh when the displayed reading is stale. Skin Temperature is a deliberate exception: its headline value always stays phone-sourced (only its trend chart recomputes on-watch). A source explicitly selected on the phone but missing among the watch's own HealthKit sources falls back to the seeded value rather than silently widening to all sources; likewise, under an All Sources selection the watch recomputes only when it can see every source the phone aggregates (otherwise the seeded values stand). If the phone hasn't reconnected long enough that the seed's re-query window (its data watermark minus the two-day re-fetch overlap) would reach past the watch's ~week of HealthKit retention — in practice a seed about 5 days old — on-watch compute is disabled and the watch shows the last snapshot the phone pushed. Manual watch verification is pending: iPhone→watch metric sync over WatchConnectivity, on-watch compute itself (see M102–M108 below), the watch dashboard ordering (the Training Load card appears first), the per-metric show/hide toggles on the watch Settings screen (hiding a metric removes its card from the home screen, an "All Metrics Hidden" state appears when none remain visible, toggling back on restores the card, and the choice persists across launches), the live HR/HRV refresh when the displayed reading is stale (including that a workout-only iPhone refresh does not mark the watch fresh, and that returning the watch app to the foreground re-checks staleness), the new metric detail pages (tapping a metric card — or a metric complication on the watch face — opens a full-screen detail view for that metric, with swipe up/down or Digital Crown paging between every metric's detail; each page is tinted in the metric's color, with the title top-right, the last 7 days as a line chart — tinted line, a ringed dot per day, today's dot solid only when today has a reading, faint per-day gridlines, weekday labels — and the current value large at the bottom-left; Readiness and Training Load show the status level beside the value, e.g. "85 · HIGH", and today's status band, the colored value range with top/bottom stripes, behind the line; the Sleep page borrows the same shape for its score, leading with it and reading the night's duration beside it ("85 pts · 7h 32m") — with the iPhone's Show Sleep Score setting off, or on a scoreless night, the duration reads large on its own as before; the chart's sleep values read in hours and skin temperature in the user's temperature unit; a day with no reading breaks the line, and a snapshot without a weekly series shows a "No recent data yet" state; a metric complication tapped for a metric not in the snapshot — before the first iPhone sync, or after its category is disabled on the phone even when other metrics remain — opens a "No Data Yet" state instead of a blank or wrong page, and if the currently open detail page's metric is dropped by a phone update mid-visit while other metrics remain, the pager snaps without animation back to the originally opened metric instead of rendering a blank page), the ring complications (accessory circular + rectangular + corner) for each metric — including that the new corner complication renders as a curved gauge hugging the bezel on a corner-style face (e.g. Infograph), showing the metric value with the arc fill and tint tracking the metric, and the top-left Home refresh button (pulls fresh Heart Rate / HRV and triggers an on-watch recompute, showing a spinner while it runs). Known limitation: complications update when the watch app runs — snapshots pushed or computed while it's closed are applied on next launch.
