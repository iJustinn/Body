# Body Test Plan

Generated 2026-07-09 against branch `body-0.9.8` (app version 0.9.8 build 8).

> Apple Watch targets (`BodyWatch`, `BodyWatchWidgetExtension`) and the shared `BodyWatchShared` group are new in 0.9.3. `ProjectConfigurationTests` asserts their configuration (bundle identifiers, versions, App Group + HealthKit entitlements); behavior is not yet covered by automated cases. The watch is display-only: it shows the metrics snapshot the iPhone computes and pushes over WatchConnectivity, and independently refreshes only Heart Rate / HRV from its own HealthKit when that snapshot is stale. Manual watch verification is pending: iPhone→watch metric sync over WatchConnectivity, the watch dashboard ordering (the Training Load card appears first), the per-metric show/hide toggles on the watch Settings screen (hiding a metric removes its card from the home screen, an "All Metrics Hidden" state appears when none remain visible, toggling back on restores the card, and the choice persists across launches), the live HR/HRV refresh when the snapshot is stale (including that a workout-only iPhone refresh does not mark the watch fresh, and that returning the watch app to the foreground re-checks staleness), the new metric detail pages (tapping a metric card — or a metric complication on the watch face — opens a full-screen detail view for that metric, with swipe up/down or Digital Crown paging between every metric's detail; each page is tinted in the metric's color, with the title top-right, the last 7 days as a line chart — tinted line, a ringed dot per day, today's dot solid only when today has a reading, faint per-day gridlines, weekday labels — and the current value large at the bottom-left; Readiness and Training Load show the status level beside the value, e.g. "85 · HIGH", and today's status band, the colored value range with top/bottom stripes, behind the line; sleep reads in hours and skin temperature in the user's temperature unit; a day with no reading breaks the line, and a snapshot without a weekly series shows a "No recent data yet" state; a metric complication tapped for a metric not in the snapshot — before the first iPhone sync, or after its category is disabled on the phone even when other metrics remain — opens a "No Data Yet" state instead of a blank or wrong page), the ring complications (accessory circular + rectangular + corner) for each metric — including that the new corner complication renders as a curved gauge hugging the bezel on a corner-style face (e.g. Infograph), showing the metric value with the arc fill and tint tracking the metric, and the top-left Home refresh button (pulls a fresh Heart Rate / HRV, showing a spinner while it runs). Known limitation: complications update when the watch app runs — snapshots pushed while it's closed are applied on next launch.
