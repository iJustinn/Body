# Body Radar Beta 3 analysis

September 23, 2026. Companion to [BodyRadarBeta3Plan.md](../BodyRadarBeta3Plan.md) (handoff checklist) and [BodyRadarBeta2Plan.md](../BodyRadarBeta2Plan.md) (Beta 1 investigation and Beta 2 decisions). Every number below was regenerated by `Scripts/body_radar_replay.py` (Python 3, standard library only) from the raw exports, which stay in `~/Downloads` and are not copied into the repository.

**Read this first.** Neither timeline here is Body's real input. The Oura timeline uses Oura's own nightly measurements. The Apple timeline uses a daily aggregate CSV (resting HR standing in for sleeping HR, daily SDNN for sleep window SDNN), not Body's sleep window inputs. Every Apple number is a daily aggregate proxy. Body's true nightly inputs remain unknown until the new exporter (Settings › Data › Cache › Export Body Radar Replay) produces files that `replay --body` can read.

## 1. Commands

All commands run from the repository root. Paths are the author's; adjust as needed.

```sh
OURA=~/Downloads/oura-data
APPLE=~/Downloads/health_data_2026-06-01_to_2026-09-06.csv
python3 Scripts/body_radar_replay.py inventory --oura "$OURA" --apple "$APPLE" [--spread-days]
python3 Scripts/body_radar_replay.py replay --rules bg --oura "$OURA" --labels Scripts/body_radar_labels.csv --from 2026-07-23 --to 2026-09-12
python3 Scripts/body_radar_replay.py replay --rules bg --apple "$APPLE" --from 2026-06-15 --to 2026-07-20
python3 Scripts/body_radar_replay.py compare --labels Scripts/body_radar_labels.csv --oura "$OURA" --apple "$APPLE" --body-chart Scripts/body_radar_body_chart_inferred.csv
python3 Scripts/body_radar_replay.py grid --oura "$OURA" --labels Scripts/body_radar_labels.csv [--out grid.csv]
python3 Scripts/body_radar_replay.py oura-extras --oura "$OURA" --labels Scripts/body_radar_labels.csv
python3 Scripts/body_radar_replay.py replay --rules bg --body BodyRadarReplay-*.json   # once exports exist
python3 -m unittest Scripts.test_body_radar_replay   # importer regression tests (Missing Sleep rows stay unscored)
```

`--rules` takes `beta2`, `b` (respiratory scored on absolute deviation), `g` (corroboration gate) or `bg` (both). `compare` runs all four unless `--rules` narrows it.

### 4.5 Candidate grid: a narrowed negative result

Regenerate with:

```sh
python3 Scripts/body_radar_replay.py grid --oura ~/Downloads/oura-data --labels Scripts/body_radar_labels.csv --out grid.csv
```

- **Ranges:** floors {Beta 2, low (HR 1.5, RR 0.3, temperature 0.1, HRV 5)} × dead zone {0.25, 0.5} × RR absolute {no, yes} × temperature weight {1, 1.5} × temporal operator {single night; sum over k nights; EWMA with λ = 1 − 1/k; k ∈ {2, 3, 4, 5, 7}} × lag {0, 1, 2} × Minor {0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2} × Major {2, 3, 4, 6} with Major above Minor. **10,032 candidates** = 16 evidence variants × 11 temporal operators × 3 lags × 19 threshold pairs.
- **Preprocessing and gaps:** nightly evidence as Beta 2 with the listed floors, dead zone, RR mode and temperature weight. Sum uses the calendar-day window [D−k+1, D]; unscored days add 0. EWMA decays once per calendar day (unscored days feed 0). The verdict on D uses the aggregate at D − lag and is evaluated only on Beta 2 eligible nights. Major = aggregate ≥ Major threshold with no flag count (more permissive than production).
- **Objective:** exact agreement (No, Minor or Major) on the 37 labeled eligible dates. Ties: fewer alerts on inferred No-signs dates, then more explicit hits, then fewer total alerts.

Results:

- Best agreement is **32/37**, equal to "always No signs" (32/37). No candidate improves on the trivial answer.
- 2,325 candidates alert on all five explicit dates, and 324 of them match all five levels exactly. Every one of them also alerts on **at least 7** of the 32 inferred No-signs dates. The best (low floors, dead zone 0.5, temperature weight 1.5, sum over 4 nights, lag 2, Minor 1.5, Major 3) reaches 30/37 with 31 alerts across 76 scored nights, alerting on Jul 31, Aug 1, 2, 3, Aug 23, Sep 2 and Sep 5.
- Fewest alerts on inferred No-signs dates by number of explicit hits: 5 hits, 7; 4 hits, 5; 3 hits, 3; 2 hits, 2; 1 or 0 hits, 0.

| Rolling sums or means (2 to 7 nights) and EWMA | Rejected | Same grid result; they smear strain into later quiet nights (Jul 31 to Aug 3, Aug 23, Sep 2, Sep 5). |
| Lagged models (lag 1 or 2) | Rejected | Lag 2 fits the two explicit episodes but fires after Jul 29, where Oura shows No signs. A deliberate delay would also make Body report yesterday's strain as today's. |
| Keep Beta 2 unchanged | Acceptable fallback | The prospective protocol (section 9) returns to Beta 2 if the gate loses real episodes. |

## 6. Selected Beta 3 rules: B + G

Implemented in app version 1.1.3 build 1 with `algorithmVersion = 3`. All Beta 2 constants are unchanged: 56 day window [D−56, D), exclude the last 3 days only when at least 28 older values remain, minimum 14, spread = max(1.4826 · MAD, floor) with floors HR 3, RR 0.6, temperature 0.2, HRV 5; d = clamp((v − median)/(2 · spread), −3, 3); weights temperature 1.5, others 1; dead zone 0.5; flag when directional d > 1; Minor at 0.75; Major at 2 with at least 2 flags; sufficiency of at least 2 channels with a baseline and at least 7 of the channel's own days in [D−13, D], plus at least 2 finite current values.

New:

- **B:** `directionalDeviation` for respiratory rate is `abs(deviation)`; the signed deviation still drives the arrow.
- **G:** `Tuning.familyContributionMinimum = 0.25`; families autonomic (HR + HRV contributions summed), respiratory, thermal. `Tuning.persistenceEvidence = minorEvidence = 0.75`. A raw Minor or Major is kept when (a) two or more families each reach 0.25, or (b) the previous calendar day's raw night is scored with raw evidence of at least 0.75 (one day lookback, recomputed from the current cache; a missing or unscored previous day breaks it). Otherwise the state is No signs with raw evidence and signals kept, and `corroboration` records `sameNight`, `persistence` or `none`.

## 7. Known disagreements

- **0/5 explicit Oura dates** alert under B+G on either proxy. The gate only suppresses; it cannot create the Aug 20 to 22 and Sep 3 episodes, whose current-night evidence is near zero.
- **Aug 31 and Sep 1 are not suppressed.** Aug 31 has two families above 0.25 (autonomic 0.85, thermal 1.09) and Sep 1 inherits persistence. Oura shows No signs on both. They follow the Aug 30 time zone change from Asia/Shanghai to America/New_York, so a travel related temperature shift is plausible; time zone shift suppression is deferred.
- **Sep 3 Major without individual callouts** is not reproduced; Beta 3 keeps the two-flag Major rule.
- Beta 2's three autonomic-only Oura Majors (Jun 19, Jun 30, Jul 3) become held nights. They fall outside the labeled window, so it is unknown whether Oura alerted on them.

## 8. Migration

Version 2 records are filtered by `isCurrentAlgorithm` when version 3 first runs. Only the 21-night chart range is backfilled from cached inputs, and version 2 verdicts cannot be exported first because 1.1.2 build 8 has no exporter. Older retained records (up to 60 nights) disappear rather than being relabeled. Nights inside the chart range whose inputs are no longer cached stay unscored.

## 9. Prospective protocol

1. Constants are frozen as in section 6 for the observation period.
2. Weekly, export from Settings › Data › Cache › Export Body Radar Replay and run `replay --rules bg --body <file>` (and `--rules beta2` for the parity baseline). Record the file hash with the date.
3. Compare each week's recomputed verdicts with Oura's Symptom Radar chart for the same days, capturing screenshots of any Oura alert (explicit labels) and noting chart No-signs segments as inferred labels with their provenance.
4. Watch specifically for a held night that Oura alerts on, and for an alert on a day Oura shows No signs.
5. Observe for at least four weeks; if no informative episode (an Oura or Body alert) occurs, extend the period. A quiet period cannot show improved detection.
6. Return to Beta 2 scoring if the prospective period shows the gate losing real episodes.
